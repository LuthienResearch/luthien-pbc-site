About Luthien

Luthien is a public benefit corporation. Our mission is to give auditors, both internal and third-party, and AI safety/security researchers superior tools to understand AI agent activity, identify harmful or misaligned behavior, and assess whether control measures are good enough to prevent harm. Our tools are open by default, designed to run in the user's own environment, and work for time-constrained audits.

We built the open-source Luthien Proxy for Claude Code, and since June 2026 we have worked with Redwood Research on Control Tower, its leaderboard, onboarding and documentation. Read our 2026 Theory of Change for the full picture.

Why this role matters

For investigation, it is difficult to reliably work out what agents did. The evidence is too large, split across coordinating swarms of agents, sometimes deliberately obscured, and possibly incomplete or altered. For testing, control evaluations don't reflect how agents are deployed inside the labs, so controls appear stronger than they actually are.

You would be one of our first full-time technical hires, and you would help set how we choose, build and ship these tools.

What you'll do

  • Propose projects in short written briefs, or join one that is already underway.
  • Run 4-week sprints with weekly feedback from the project's customer, such as an auditor or an AI control researcher, ending in a review where users try the work.
  • Build tools that run in the auditor's own environment and test them against benchmarks with known answers.
  • Publish methods, results and code openly, so independent researchers and developers can build on them.

Projects in your first few months

These four briefs are the leading candidates for our first sprints.

  • Auditor tooling (investigation). Measure which ways of presenting agent logs, chain of thought, and tool calls help an auditor catch sabotage most often, using Redwood's Auditing Sabotage Bench as the answer key.
  • Rogue incident detection (investigation). Turn the methods Asymmetric Security, Transluce, Nightingale, and SwarmTraces used to find rogue agent activity in public records into automated detections.
  • Harness red-teaming (testing). Build a kit that runs Redwood's harness attacks against Claude Code and Codex across real configurations and reports which attacks got past the safeguards.
  • Loss of control threat model for Lab auditors. Place every Lab incident disclosed in 2026 on a severity scale and list the checks an auditor should run at each level.

Who we're looking for

We're looking for a researcher who builds, or a builder who does research:

  • Researcher turned builder. You have done AI safety, security, evaluation or ML research, and you want your work to become tools that other people use.
  • Builder who does research. You ship production software, and you are comfortable reading papers, designing experiments and measuring whether something works.

Either way, you have hands-on experience with LLMs or AI agents, you use frontier AI coding tools fluently, and you are motivated to reduce risk from advanced AI. Non-traditional backgrounds are welcome.

What you get

  • Direct work with auditors and AI control researchers, the people our tools are for.
  • Work that is open by default: your methods, results and code are published.
  • A say in what we build: anyone on the team can propose a project.

This role depends on Luthien's funding. We will tell you plainly where that stands when we talk.
